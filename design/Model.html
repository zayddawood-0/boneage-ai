Limitations

  • Trained on X-rays from a small number of US hospitals; accuracy may differ for other populations.
  • Labels follow radiologist bone age readings, so the model inherits their variation.
  • The normal-range threshold used by the agent is a setting, not something the model learns.
  • Not for children outside the age range of the training data.
External check: evaluate on the Digital Hand Atlas or RHPE datasets to see how the model does on other populations.
